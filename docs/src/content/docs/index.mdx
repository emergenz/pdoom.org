**crowd-cast** is a desktop application that records paired **screencast video** and
**keyboard/mouse input** for the applications you choose, building a
dataset of real human computer work for research. It runs quietly in your menu
bar (macOS) or system tray, captures only the apps you select (see
[Privacy & data](/docs/crowd-cast/privacy/)), pauses when you step away, and
uploads finished segments in the background.

It's a single binary with [libobs](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio)
embedded, so there's nothing else to install.

Want to contribute your own work to the dataset? Participants are paid for their
recordings; see [paid data collection](/careers/paid-data-collection/).

## Get running in under five minutes

1. Install the app for your platform.
2. Walk through the first-run setup: grant permissions, pick the apps to record, and sign in.
3. You're recording. Manage everything from [the tray menu](/docs/crowd-cast/tray/).

## Learn more

The [crowd-cast paper](https://pdoom.org/crowd_cast.html) covers the research
behind the project and how the data is used. The
[dashboard](https://pdoom.org/crowd_cast_dashboard.html) shows your contributions
and overall collection progress.
